# Astra: Keeper of the Meridian

An original lens-routing defense game. Place four distinct beacons along the old road, build an economy by clearing shadows, and hold the Meridian through ten escalating waves and an Eclipse Heart boss. Upgrade, sell back, pause, and change the pace of the watch.

## How to Play

Select a lens with the buttons or keys 1–4, then click open ground to place it. The watch begins with 720 light. Beacons attack automatically; select one to upgrade with U or sell with X. Launch each wave with Space or the launch button. P pauses, F toggles speed, and M mutes synthesized sound. Keep the heart's lives above zero through wave 10.
